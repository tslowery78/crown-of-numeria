Homework problems (optional)

One problem per line: question | answer. For word answers add choices: Is 17 even or odd? | odd | even; odd. These come first, then the game makes more practice problems. Mixed numbers such as 1 1/2 work too; enter 3/2 or 1.5 in the game. Use ± for negative answers.
