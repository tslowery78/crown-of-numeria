# Playing Crown of Numeria on a Meta Quest 3

There's nothing to install. The game runs in the Quest's own **Browser** app from a web address.

## One-time setup (about 10 minutes)

1. **Clear a play space.** Clear an empty area, ideally at least 2 × 2 m (6½ × 6½ ft), with nothing to trip over.
2. **Set up a room-scale boundary.** On the Quest go to **Settings → Boundary** and choose **Roomscale**, then trace the clear floor. Keep the boundary turned on: the Quest shows it automatically in the game when a player gets near the edge.
3. **(Optional) Turn on hand tracking.** Go to **Settings → Movement tracking → Hand tracking**. The game works with controllers or bare hands.
4. **Open the Browser.** From the app library (the grid icon on the menu bar), open **Browser**.
5. **Type the player's link** in the address bar, replacing `NAME` with her first name:
   - Big sister (4th-grade maths): `tslowery78.github.io/crown-of-numeria/?player=NAME&grade=4&look=older`
   - Little sister (2nd-grade maths): `tslowery78.github.io/crown-of-numeria/?player=NAME&grade=2&look=younger`
   - Grown-ups: `…?player=NAME&grade=4&look=mom` (or `look=dad`)

   The name stays on the headset only. It never goes into the public code.
6. **Bookmark it.** Use the Browser menu (⋮) → **Add bookmark**. Repeat steps 5–6 for each player.
7. **(Optional) Grown-up settings.** On the setup page, open **Grown-up settings** to choose her Bluebonnet modules, paste in this week's homework, and set the **Play area** to the size of your clear space. Pick the next size down; 2.5 m is the default.

## Every time she plays

1. Put the headset on and open **Browser → Bookmarks → her name**.
2. If she stopped part-way last time, leave **Continue the climb** selected.
3. **Stand in the middle of the play space and face the wall where the Magic Lock should be.**
4. Tap **Enter the Castle in VR**. If the Quest asks to allow immersive mode, choose **Allow**.

## Controls (shown on the welcome sign too)

- **Buttons:** touch them with a fingertip or the tip of the magic wand, or point and pull the trigger.
- **Pick up and throw:** pinch or use the grip button near an object, and let go to throw.
- **Go up a floor:** after unlocking the Magic Lock, stand on the glowing square in the middle.
- **Magic Scroll:** write on it with a fingertip (or point and hold the trigger). **Keep page** saves the page for printing.
- **Recenter:** hold the Meta button. The castle stays on the same spot; if the Quest asks, walk back to the middle and pinch.
- **Stop playing:** press the Meta button and choose **Quit** (or take the headset off). Progress is saved.

## After playing (grown-ups)

Open her bookmark and tap **grown-up settings or homework**:

- **Homework report:** every problem, her tries, and how long each took.
- **Answer-booklet inserts:** print small cut-out cards of her Magic Scroll working. If the Quest can't print, use **Save as picture**.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "VR not available here" | Make sure the page is open in the Quest's **Browser** (not the phone app), and the address starts `tslowery78.github.io`. Update the Quest if the Browser is old. |
| The castle is off-centre or she walks through a wall | Quit, stand in the middle facing the Magic Lock wall, and enter again. Also check the Play area size isn't bigger than the clear space. |
| Too-close-to-the-edge warning | Step back towards the middle; it clears by itself. |
| No sound or read-aloud | Turn up the Quest volume. Read-aloud needs the Quest's text-to-speech voice, and some headsets don't have one. |
| Screen freezes or goes black | Press the Meta button, quit, and open the bookmark again. **Continue the climb** puts her back on her floor. |
